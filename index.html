About me

I didn't take a straight line to analytics, and that's my advantage.

It started in India, where I became a computer engineer and wrote my first real code as a backend web developer.

Then I moved to the UK. As a Shift Leader and Operations Manager in London, I led a team that made the business proud, and learned that every number on a report reflects the people behind the work.

Wanting to go further, I came to Canada to pursue a Master of Applied Computing at the University of Windsor.

Today I work at Vistaprint as a Manufacturing Analyst, supporting multiple plants with reports they can trust. What I value most is the impact: every morning, plant leaders start their day with a report I built that shows how their whole plant is performing.

The next chapter starts in January 2027, and I'd love to write it with your team.
